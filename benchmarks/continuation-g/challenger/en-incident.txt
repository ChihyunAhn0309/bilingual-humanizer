Tuesday's CSV export incident began at 14:20 UTC. Projects with more than 10,000 records received incomplete files; smaller projects were unaffected, and PDF exports kept working. At 14:47 we switched off CSV export rather than let customers download partial data, and at 15:05 we updated the status page.

We traced the fault to a pagination change released Monday and rolled it back at 16:10. Checks of 25 previously affected projects then returned complete row counts in every case, so we restored CSV export at 16:35. We still don't know why the pre-release load test missed the fault. By Friday, the platform team will add a test case above 10,000 records and report whether the other paginated exports need equivalent coverage.

Customers do not otherwise need to act. If you downloaded a CSV between 14:20 and 14:47, please export it again.
