At 14:20 UTC on Tuesday, the export service began returning incomplete CSV files for projects with more than 10,000 records. Projects below that threshold were not affected, and PDF exports continued to work. We disabled CSV export at 14:47 rather than risk customers downloading partial data. The status page was updated at 15:05.

The team traced the issue to a pagination change released on Monday. We rolled that change back at 16:10, then checked 25 previously affected projects and found complete row counts in all 25. CSV export was restored at 16:35. We have not yet determined why the pre-release load test missed the fault. By Friday, the platform team will add a case above 10,000 records and will report whether other paginated exports need the same coverage. No customer action is required, but anyone who downloaded a CSV between 14:20 and 14:47 should export it again.
