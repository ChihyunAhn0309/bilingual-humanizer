# Compose English around what the writer means to say

Use for substantial English rewriting within the existing draft/revision budget. The shared meaning map is a coverage constraint, not a sentence plan. Do not turn each map item into an equally weighted sentence or produce a tidier version of the source's cadence.

## Make a brief that can produce prose

Decide what this writer is doing for this reader: recommending, explaining, questioning, recalling, evaluating, or another source-supported act. Keep the writer's particular stance. A mixed review is not a neutral inventory; a cautious recommendation is not a confident decision. Preserve a few useful source expressions or voice cues rather than reducing everything to impersonal facts. With no sample, do not claim a distinctive author match.

Give each paragraph a focal relationship, such as a recommendation and its operating constraint, or an exception and why it changes the writer's judgment. Distinguish the point the reader should grasp from its support, qualification and next action. All remain content, but they need not receive equal grammatical emphasis or a sentence each. The relationship must come from the source; do not manufacture a cause or a rebuttal to create momentum.

A brief can be very short: **writer's act and stance; reader's next understanding; focal relationships; protected facts and exact material; voice anchors.** Include every dependency needed for fidelity. This is a drafting aid, not an extra deliverable or a new framework the user must configure.

## Draft a connected passage

Compose the whole passage from that brief. Let the main point govern the main clause, and attach supporting detail where the reader needs it. Choose sentence subjects and verbs for the actual relationship rather than keeping every source sentence's subject. Keep a limitation with the claim it restricts, and keep a recommendation distinct from its evidence. Preserve chronology or suspense when the source depends on it.

For example, a review can keep the value of the overall visit together while discussing a paid add-on's strengths and price judgment separately. A planning note can put a staffing constraint next to its recommendation while preserving an attendance advantage and the limits of the records. These are possible relationships, not templates or instructions to make all reviews and notes follow the same order.

Read the complete wording sequentially as the intended reader. Notice where reference, a strained phrase, a needless explanation or a sudden shift makes the reader reconstruct the point. Repair that passage. Do not claim an audible read-aloud or human listener unless one actually occurred. Ordinary verbs and contractions can fit professional English; the absence of contractions in a source is not a ban. Formal provisions, quotations and explicit style guides still govern. No slang, invented reactions, forced fragments or sentence-length targets are needed.

Reopen the original for a separate fidelity pass. Check the relationships as well as the facts: who knows what, what remains uncertain, what caused what, what is merely possible, and what the writer is asking or promising. A fluent sentence can change the scope of a small connective. An anticipated outcome is not a desired one; missing evidence for a claim is not proof the event never occurred. Restore lost information by recomposing its paragraph, not by tacking caveats onto the end. Complete the final reader review and the existing detector-feedback loop.

## Use feedback to choose the next action

A review finding needs an exact passage and a reader or fidelity reason. Fix accepted findings and check them on the completed revision. Retain a valid genre convention or deliberate source expression; do not rename it as another defect. A low native Human value does not identify a bad sentence.

For an explicitly requested stronger complete-composition comparison, choose a different useful focal relationship or reader route under [composition-variants.md](composition-variants.md). State its purpose before drafting. The alternative may trade immediate access to a recommendation for seeing its evidence first; it need not be universally better. Keep the best faithful eligible complete text, and preserve lower-scoring and rejected alternatives. Do not add attempts or increase the budget merely because the score is disappointing.

When another writer context is available and delegation is authorized, one such alternative may use **brief-only drafting**: send the complete meaning/voice brief and protected exact material to a fresh writer without the original prose, previous drafts, scores or desired verdict. Ask for one complete composition. The source stays with the fidelity reviewer, who must compare the returned text against it before scoring. Include all uncertainty, agency and speech acts in the brief; a compressed fact list that loses them is not sufficient. Count the returned candidate and any repair in the same existing budget. Record that the context changed: this tests a workflow, not just wording instructions. In a single context, drafting from notes does not erase the source from memory; report that limit rather than claiming a fresh writer. Do not create a separate user-owned chat to simulate this option.

## Respect genre and voice

For academic English, preserve each section's actual job: a stated problem in an introduction is not a result; prior authors' findings stay attributed; methods retain sequence and conditions; results keep denominators, baselines and uncertainty; discussion marks interpretation as interpretation. Do not impose a new paper template, remove necessary procedural repetition or invent future work.

Infer sample features separately. Dictation may support cadence without establishing punctuation; a jointly edited report need not establish personal tone. Needed signposts, technical repetition, genuine contrasts and real conclusions remain useful. A broad statement need not acquire a fabricated statistic or anecdote to sound specific. Follow [english.md](english.md) for local choices and [genres-and-voice.md](genres-and-voice.md) when the genre requires them.

The primary editorial sources and their limits are recorded in [english-reference-review.md](english-reference-review.md). These are practical drafting hypotheses, not implementations of a proprietary humanizer or evidence of general detector acceptance.
