I recommend retaining the Wednesday repair session during the winter rather than moving it to Saturday. This recommendation is based on the attendance records from the last six sessions, rather than on a survey of everyone who might want to attend. Wednesday attendance ranged from 11 to 18 people. The two Saturday trials attracted 20 and 23 people, but each required a second volunteer at the electrical bench.

Higher attendance is welcome, although it does not by itself resolve the staffing question. Mara can continue to cover Wednesdays until the end of February. She has not committed to Saturdays, and we do not yet have another qualified volunteer. Moving the session now could therefore leave the electrical bench unavailable even when more visitors arrive.

I suggest reviewing the decision in January if another volunteer comes forward. In the meantime, we should record how many people leave without a repair because they cannot wait. We have counted completed repairs so far, but those totals cannot tell us whether Wednesday is convenient for people who have not attended.
