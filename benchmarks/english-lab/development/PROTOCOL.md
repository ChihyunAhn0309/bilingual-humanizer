# English development experiment

Both sources were generated by the English development agent on 2026-10-03 as new AI-origin synthetic fixtures. Neither describes authenticated people, transactions, visits or institutions. They are development material, not human controls or untouched test data.

Scope: two different genres of complete prose, 120-200 words apiece. Six detector invocations planned (unchanged source, initial draft, one revision/composition alternative per case); at most two additional calls only for a justified repair or informative alternative. One initial draft plus no more than three revisions per case. Each actual changed post-draft candidate counts, including rejected unscored repairs. English inference is serial, offline, and performed only by this agent with the staged local_feedback.py adapter and the installed detector v3.1.2. No detector/model changes, paid services, hosted inference, or new factual content.

Hypothesis: a proposition list can accidentally flatten a writer's main point, supporting facts and limits into equally weighted sentences. Plan the writer's speech act and paragraph focus, keep source-backed voice anchors, and subordinate supporting facts to the actual point. Compose continuous paragraphs from that hierarchy, then compare with the original for full information and modality. This is a drafting hypothesis, not demonstrated detector improvement.

Distinguish three public/free editorial methods reviewed: Thomas and Turner's classic-style stance centres what the writer wants a competent reader to see; UW-Madison's reverse outline separates paragraph subject from function; UNC's read-aloud method tests the exact completed language from a listener's limited ability to backtrack. These are editorial sources, not humanizer algorithms. No audio reading is claimed unless a real audio tool is used. The experiment uses a silent sequential read for idiom and reference.

Primary sources retrieved 2026-10-03:
- https://classicprose.com/csx.html — selectively adopt reader-directed presentation; reject importing the style's certainty/disinterested stance when the source expresses uncertainty or advocacy.
- https://writing.wisc.edu/handbook/processandstructure/reverseoutlines/ — inspect actual paragraph functions after drafting; retain all substantive source content rather than treating reorganization as permission to cut it.
- https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/reading-aloud/ — exact-word read-through for awkwardness and order; no forced conversational register and no claim that silent model review equals audible human listening.

Baseline source measurement and validated whole-document evidence/triage precede each first draft. Each candidate receives separate fidelity and reader review before scoring, then a fresh whole-document review with actual measurements. Independent review is requested from the parent; self-review is labeled as such. Keep all raw receipts, inputs, rejected candidates and native four-class probabilities. Select a faithful reader-eligible complete candidate; report score gains and failures separately from editorial judgments.
