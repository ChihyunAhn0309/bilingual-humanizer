## What changed

Only SKILL.md, references/english-composition.md and references/english-reference-review.md changed in the staged prototype. Its scripts and existing iteration limits are unchanged. The English composition guide was reduced from 1,227 words to a more focused drafting method:

1. Treat the meaning map as a coverage constraint, not one finished sentence per fact.
2. Preserve the writer's actual speech act and stance; identify the relationship each paragraph should make clear.
3. Give the main point, its evidence, its limits and its next action different grammatical roles while retaining every one of them.
4. Compose connected prose, then perform a separate exact-source comparison and sequential reader pass before the existing detector-feedback loop.

The policy draft attaches the staffing trade-off directly to the recommendation. The review separates the value of the exhibition from the value of the paid guide, places the interview recommendation beside what it helped the writer understand, and gives the price verdict its supporting context. Independent parent pre-score reviews found these complete candidates faithful and useful; the parent knew the method and source scores, so these were not blind human assessments.

The primary editorial methods inspected were Thomas and Turner's author-hosted classic-style excerpt, UW-Madison's paragraph subject/function reverse outline, and UNC's read-aloud guidance. They inform a reader-oriented method, not a detector-evasion algorithm. Exact links, adopted ideas and rejected assumptions are in [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) and the staged English reference review. No audible read-aloud is claimed.

## What failed or needed repair

The policy evidence-first alternative did not outperform the selected draft. It also initially turned a limitation on the recommendation's evidence into a claim about whether a survey had been conducted; the unscored text and correction remain saved.

The first exhibition draft changed uncertainty about the cause of a mismatch into uncertainty about exhibit movement itself. Parent review caught this before scoring. A later fresh-context writer saw only a meaning-and-voice brief, but changed expectation into hope and current uncertainty into past inability. After repairs, that route scored 0.1896229%, well below 25.0669241%. Brief-only fresh-context drafting was therefore **removed from the recommended method**, with its failed proposal retained in [brief-only-prototype-not-selected.md](brief-only-prototype-not-selected.md). A new context did not establish a better composition or prevent semantic drift.

A coordination race produced two parallel repairs of that last exhibition draft. Both exact texts are preserved; only the parent's approved fresh-writer version was scored. Five distinct exhibition draft artifacts were created instead of the planned four. The [provenance correction](exhibition-review/draft-4-provenance-correction.md) records the discrepancy. The main sequence uses three revisions and passes the existing cycle validator, but that pass does not certify compliance with the total artifact ceiling. No budget reset or extra authorization is claimed, and no additional detector call was spent on the parallel repair.
