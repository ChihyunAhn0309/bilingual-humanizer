The harbour exhibition was worth the visit on Sunday. I spent about an hour there and would have stayed longer if the upstairs room had been open. The ticket desk didn't say when it would reopen.

I hired the audio guide for £4, expecting it to explain how the port had changed. Most recordings described objects the labels had already identified. One six-minute interview, though, helped me understand a photograph I'd walked past: a former crane operator explained why crews used hand signals when the radio failed. I'd recommend that interview.

The guide was easy to use. I could replay a recording without starting the whole sequence again, but the numbers on the cases didn't always follow the guide's order. I doubled back twice. I don't know whether the mismatch was permanent or resulted from exhibits being moved. Even with the interview, I wouldn't hire the guide again at its current price.
