I visited the harbour exhibition on Sunday and hired the audio guide for £4. I expected it to explain how the port had changed, but most of the recordings described objects already identified on the labels. The exception was a six-minute interview with a former crane operator. He explained why crews used hand signals when the radio failed, and that account helped me understand a photograph I had previously walked past.

The guide was easy to operate, and I could replay an individual recording without restarting the whole sequence. However, the numbering on the cases did not always match the order in the guide. I doubled back twice, although I cannot tell whether this was a permanent problem or the result of exhibits being moved.

I would recommend the interview, but I would not hire the guide again at its current price. The exhibition itself was worth the visit. I spent about an hour there, and I would have stayed longer if the upstairs room had been open. The ticket desk did not say when it would reopen.
