# Independent English composition development

The user requested an independent session to improve English humanizing itself using the existing AI detector's feedback and repeated rewriting. The development lead starts with fresh context and owns the proposed drafting method. The parent coordinates independent pre-score review, later testing, preservation and publication. All participants are AI agents; separate context is not human or cross-model validation.

## Frozen starting point

Production is v1.10.0, with its exact 33-file skill snapshot in `baseline-skill` and hashes in `baseline-skill-hashes.json`. Earlier high-scoring texts and all published records remain unchanged. The installed detector and its originating session both identify v3.1.2 as latest. Its existing pinned English model and four-class interpretation are fixed throughout. There is no paid API, commercial scan, new service account or hosted inference in this experiment.

## Development and feedback

The independent lead creates two distinct synthetic English development sources and investigates a concise alternative drafting method. These are explicitly AI-authored fixtures, not evidence of real experiences or transactions. For each, score and review the unchanged source first. Read the actual native result alongside the detector skill's full paragraph review and grounded counterexplanations. State the proposed reader improvement before rewriting. The parent, who did not write the candidates, compares each with its source and saves eligibility before rescoring. Feed the new measured result and whole-document review back to the writer, then revise justified issues or test a declared useful composition alternative. Preserve all outcomes, including rejection and regression.

Plan six development measurements (two sources, two first drafts and two follow-ups), with at most two further informative runs if justified. The existing per-document initial draft plus three-revision ceiling remains. Reusing an older document would also inherit its earlier revision use. Windows English inference is serial; the development lead owns inference first, then explicitly transfers it to the parent. No model, calibration, wording history or raw percentage is changed to obtain a favourable result.

## Fresh-source transfer check

A separate evaluator prepares three new English sources, full source constraints, known synthetic origin and hashes without viewing the prototype or model scores. The lead cannot inspect them until its development method and exact skill files are frozen. These are fresh development transfer cases, not a representative population or a formal statistical holdout study.

The parent then measures the unchanged three sources and provides the same source/whole-document feedback to the baseline and prototype writers. The baseline writer has fresh context and uses only the v1.10 skill. The prototype writer uses the frozen proposed skill; its prior method-development context differs and must be disclosed. Each writes and reviews within the same scope. A score-blind separate evaluator compares anonymous candidates against all source constraints and assesses reader quality before candidate scoring. Any necessary fidelity repair is preserved and counted; unrepaired candidates are not scored or selected. All eligible candidates receive the same unchanged local detector measurement.

Do not retroactively tune the frozen skill against transfer scores and call the result untouched transfer evidence. If later feedback is acted on, preserve the initial comparison and label later candidates as feedback-driven continuations. Match every reported number to that exact full text. Highest faithful historical scores and highest delivery-eligible closed candidates remain separate.

## Decision and reporting

Report per-case native Human values and absolute percentage-point changes, all four classes, semantic/reader judgments, failures, real cycles and provenance. Humanized is not Human. The user's desired Human >=50 threshold remains a separate explicit outcome, not something inferred from a positive delta. The English model is uncalibrated and has known high-confidence human false positives; local results cannot establish historical authorship or commercial transfer.

A claimed general performance gain needs directional improvement beyond development cases, preserved meaning and no material reader-quality regression. A tiny relative gain near zero is not a breakthrough. Promote reusable composition changes only with supporting observed behavior, clearly distinguishing editorial benefits from measured detection benefits. If the prototype does not support a useful improvement, keep v1.10 installed and publish the negative findings and recoverable prototype instead of upgrading its performance claim. Independent final review checks the actual evidence and proposed release decision.
