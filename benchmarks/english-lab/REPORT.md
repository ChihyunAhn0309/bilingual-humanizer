# Independent English humanizer development and final decision

**Production remains v1.10.0.** An independent development session produced a shorter English composition prototype, but the frozen comparison and later feedback-driven alternatives did not demonstrate the useful general high-Human gain the user requested. Preserve the current installed skill, all previous releases/checkpoints, the experimental guide, and every favourable or unfavourable result. No new production-performance release is claimed.

The independent auditor recommends this decision after [703 integrity checks](release-audit/INDEPENDENT-FROZEN-AUDIT.md). The helper regression suite passed 106 tests with one optional dependency skip. Tests and checksums verify records and mechanics; they do not prove writing quality or authorship. [Machine-readable results](results.json) and [first comparison](transfer-comparison.json) retain exact native classes, hashes and limitations.

T01 is a public explanation, T02 an operational memo, and T03 a personal reflection. These are **native uncalibrated Human class values**, not Humanized, 100 minus AI, word shares, or authenticated authorship. The English detector is an experimental auxiliary model with known high-confidence human false positives. No first prototype draft reached 1%, let alone 50%. T03's +0.001095 percentage-point difference is not meaningful general improvement evidence. Two slight reader preferences and a tie remain useful local editorial observations, not population results.

The three sources and their constraints were independently created and frozen before the developer saw them. All 33 prototype files were frozen before source release. Both writers received identical original/native/full-review feedback. A separate reviewer checked every proposition and all 22 candidate paragraphs with method identities and candidate scores hidden, before the six candidate measurements. The prototype writer also had development context; the baseline writer had a fresh context. The evaluator authored the synthetic sources. Those differences prevent a clean causal or human-rater interpretation.

## Actual repeated feedback and retained checkpoints

The detector's complete evidence review was used, not only its number. Source review covered all 10 paragraphs and six dimensions. Post-draft review bound all six measured texts and covered all 22 paragraphs, with 44 findings explicitly retained and no invented repair quota. Both writers read and recorded decisions on the actual post-draft evidence. The existing cycle helper validates six source-to-draft feedback sessions, including recovery of an original when it scores higher.

The user-requested stronger comparison then tried a different complete reader route for T01 and T02 under the same frozen guide. Parent reviews preceded scoring; a separate evaluator subsequently reviewed both complete alternatives and native findings. T01 draft 2 scored **0.326737%**, below its earlier **0.962205%**. T02 draft 2 reached **0.631071%**, above its first prototype draft but below the original **0.671584%**. Neither showed a decisive reader advantage. Full feedback was returned to the writer again, the two final cycles were checked, and earlier eligible checkpoints were retained:

These continuations are post-feedback development, not additional untouched transfer tests. Each consumed one revision after the initial draft; the skill and first comparison stayed frozen. Returning the original for T02 is an explicit fallback, not a claimed rewrite gain.

## Development evidence and proposed method

Before transfer, two new synthetic development cases received six measurements and full feedback. The selected planning note moved from **0.195801% to 0.633648%**; the selected exhibition review moved from **0.016360% to 25.066924%**. The review's improvement is substantial on that one development text but lacks a retained-skill paired control. [Development report](development/REPORT.md) retains the other scores, meaning errors rejected before scoring and exact native outputs. No new measured text reached Human 50%.

The [experimental guide](skill/references/english-composition.md) uses the writer's act/stance and the relationships between a point, support, limitation and action as its drafting brief. The meaning map protects coverage without forcing one sentence per fact. It narrows the English guidance from 1,227 to 823 words. Three additional primary writing references complement the prior 15-repository/product review. These are editorial hypotheses, not proprietary humanizer implementations.

A brief-only fresh-writer alternative was tested, required two fidelity repairs and scored only **0.189623%** on the exhibition review. It was removed from the proposed recommended method. All variants are preserved; starting a fresh context is not itself a validated advantage.

## Deviations, limits and cost

One development repair race produced two parallel repairs of the same draft: five distinct exhibition draft artifacts instead of the planned four. Both exact versions and the overwrite/recovery history remain saved; one was unscored. Main-path cycle validity does not certify compliance with the total artifact cap. The retained exhibition draft predates this race.

After first candidates and blind judgments were frozen, the released pair/triage feedback contained some counterpart quotations. Both writers disclosed this exposure and projected later reads to their own fields. It cannot affect the already frozen first drafts, but continuation blindness is not claimed. The first independent reviewer stayed identity-blind until its final review was frozen. All participants are AI contexts; no human or cross-model rating is claimed.

**17 new document-level local submissions completed:** six development, nine first transfer (three originals plus six drafts), two continuations. Each request also ran paragraph-deletion probes; 17 is not the number of internal model forward passes. No hosted/commercial detector calls, paid services, account changes, model downloads, weight edits or calibration changes occurred. Existing Codex usage and local computing are distinct from detector-service fees. The 179 historical commercial observations are unchanged and supply no new transfer claim for this prototype.

Production's 33 skill files and both v1.10 ZIPs remain byte-identical. The [baseline archive](baseline-skill/) and [prototype freeze](METHOD-FREEZE.json) allow recovery and inspection. [Final payload review](release-audit/FINAL-PAYLOAD-REVIEW.md) checks publication evidence separately. Native receipts are retained with original Windows paths; the repository evidence verifier resolves its own portable relative paths and runs without model inference.
