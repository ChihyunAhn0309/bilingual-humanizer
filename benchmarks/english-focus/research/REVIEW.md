# English-humanizer research record

Reviewed 2026-10-03. This record distinguishes documentation, source inspection and editorial adoption from measured performance. Version 1.9.0's [English composition changes](../../../skills/bilingual-humanizer/references/english-composition.md) remain hypotheses pending validation; no quality gain, commercial detector improvement or universal ranking is claimed here.

Scope: 15 repository snapshots, selected public product/style/research pages, and an independent audit of nine repositories. Related projects share methods and examples, so repository count is not independent evidence count. No upstream code was installed/executed, no accounts or payment were used, and no rewriting/inference request was submitted to a product. Public-page descriptions do not verify actual free quota or unlimited usage.

[repositories.json](repositories.json) pins the 15 repositories, requested files, retrieval errors, SHA-256 values and commits. [independent-extra/manifest.json](independent-extra/manifest.json) records nine additional pinned reference files. The [independent audit](independent-reference-audit.md) details actual reads for nine sources and baseline comparison; its 30 assigned original snapshot files passed hash verification. Files merely downloaded are not represented as fully reviewed.

## Primary writing and research sources

- [Google developer tone guidance](https://developers.google.com/style/tone): audience-appropriate conversational language and suitable contractions inform positive English delivery. This does not justify slang, persona injection or casualizing legal/academic material.
- [Google technical editing guidance](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/two/editing): edit organization and reader understanding, then review the completed text. The skill retains the original source as its factual authority.
- [DIPPER paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.13408) and [authors' code](https://github.com/martiansideofthemoon/ai-detection-paraphrases): context-conditioned paraphrasing and lexical/order controls belong to a trained 11B system. Naming those ideas in a prompt does not reproduce training or reported performance. No model was installed or run.
- [DAMAGE](https://aclanthology.org/2025.genaidetect-1.9/): studies 19 humanizers. The authors' Pangram affiliation is relevant context; results belong to the tested products, text and settings. It is not a neutral universal ranking or validation of version 1.9.0.

## Adoption mapped to the actual draft

| English-composition section | Adopted synthesis | What remains excluded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Choose how the writer speaks | Appropriate register/contractions; recipient question first when context is available | Quotas, forced casualness, fabricated shared context |
| Re-express relationships | Proposition-based clauses, known-to-new continuity, noun attachment, focus and meaningful pauses | New causal/concessive relationships; mechanical sentence combining or length targets |
| Test completed discourse | Opening/ending sequence, concession-reset, false ranges, empty rebuttals, recoverable claims | Deleting real limitations or generalizations; inventing concrete examples |
| Academic English | Preserve distinct functions of abstract, introduction, related work, methods, results and discussion | Added literature, mechanisms, future work or stronger claims |
| Voice evidence and feedback | Feature-specific confidence, provenance and channel; exact-span repairs with deduplicated labels | Authorship certification, stereotyped persona, excessive mandatory reporting |

The baseline already protected content coverage, modality, attribution, numerical relationships, exact material, writer identity and genre. This revision operationalizes English composition within those constraints. Community rule counts, self-reported detector successes and bundled regression tests do not establish reader preference or broad efficacy. Fidelity, naturalness and detector behavior must be evaluated separately on exact final candidates; until that evaluation exists, the release describes an improved method proposal, not proven empirical gains.
