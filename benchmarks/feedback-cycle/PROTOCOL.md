# Feedback-driven continuation, v1.10

This is an English development check of the integrated feedback workflow, not a new blinded performance benchmark. The two synthetic AI-authored sources, original measurements and v1.9 initial drafts are inherited unchanged from `benchmarks/english-focus`. Reusing an unchanged measurement preserves its original timestamp; it is not a new run. Source-to-initial-draft decisions are reconstructed from the saved writer records and an explicit retrospective reviewer check, not described as an earlier automated closed loop.

The parent reads the actual native result, whole-document detector review, exact findings and counterexplanations. A separate context reviews the current drafts and identifies concrete reader problems. The parent rewrites those findings; the reviewer checks every source constraint before scoring. Each eligible changed candidate is scored serially by the unchanged local v3.1.2 detector. The same follow-up reviewer then reads the new measurement and reviews resolution, all paragraphs and new findings. This is separate from writing, but not a new blind reviewer at every round and not human evaluation.

Each accepted finding needs an exact before/after link and a checked resolved or carried outcome. A new ID or a score gain cannot erase it. The numerical adapter does not itself supply the interpretive review; the detector skill's evidence protocol governs that review. Model deletion sensitivity prioritizes inspection and never authorizes dropping facts. Every final selection must preserve the immutable source, reader quality and closure of accepted feedback. The highest faithful historical score is retained separately if its draft has open issues.

Keep the existing maximum of one initial draft plus three revisions. The support email has no recorded inherited post-draft repair. The reflection has one inherited pre-freeze fidelity repair (`beforehand` to `before the experiment`); count it as one prior revision, although the pre-repair candidate was not frozen and has no score. Do not invent that missing snapshot or reset the allowance. Further meaningful composition alternatives, if used, consume the same allowance. Editorial closure, a resource/budget stop, and attainment of a requested Human threshold are different outcomes.

Freeze every input and returned artifact. Keep scores, timestamps, class definitions, model identity and cautions intact. English Human, AI, AI-edited and Humanized remain separate. These uncalibrated local values have known false positives and do not establish historical authorship, human ratings or commercial-detector transfer. No detector weights/calibration changes, hosted inference, payments or new commercial scans are part of this phase. Prior releases and all commercial observations remain available.
