For eight weeks, I tried reading a chapter in the morning before I looked at my phone. I'd hoped to remember more of what I read. I could recall the plots of three novels at the end of those weeks, but I hadn't tested my memory before starting the experiment. I don't know whether my recall improved.

What I noticed was simpler. I stopped breaking off mid-chapter to check messages, and I liked reading that way. I still checked my phone as soon as I'd finished. On two mornings I skipped reading altogether; I didn't stick to the routine every day.

I'm reluctant to recommend it to everyone. My mornings were quiet during those weeks, and I didn't try it while travelling. Even without evidence that it helped my memory, the reading was worthwhile to me.

Next month I'd like to try reading after dinner instead. I haven't picked a start date.
