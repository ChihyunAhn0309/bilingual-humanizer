# Choose what to preserve and what to recompose

Use this for active rewriting of substantial or formulaic prose. It changes how the candidate is composed; it does not add review turns. Preserve a structure the user requires and keep a light edit within its requested scope.

Before drafting, identify both useful source features and concrete reader problems. Use **recomposition** when repeated scaffolding, dispersed conditions or a weak information order obstruct the text. Use **selective restructuring** when the source already has a clear argument, appropriate cadence or distinctive voice: preserve those spans and revise the actual weaknesses. A facts-only map is a coverage aid, not a replacement for a personal text's phrasing, scene order or emotional restraint. Choose per passage when a document mixes effective and formulaic writing.

Keep an unchanged copy for comparison. A later candidate should solve an identifiable problem without making another important reader task harder. The amount of changed wording is not evidence of improvement. A source-sensitive route is not a detector-pass technique; commercial scores can disagree even when independent reader review prefers the edit.

## 1. Separate meaning from its packaging

First set a small **editorial brief**: who is reading, what this text does for them, how directly the writer addresses them, and which source-backed voice traits matter. For example, a formal internal explanation can be direct and matter-of-fact without becoming a chatty note or inventing `we`. An essay may retain a hesitant personal judgment; an announcement may simply put the reader's practical question first. Choose these positive qualities before checking for unwanted phrases. With no author sample, use the source's stance and the genre; do not invent a persona.

Build a compact working map of the source. For each distinct point, retain:

- the proposition and its actor, object, time and scope;
- its status: fact, plan, possibility, interpretation, opinion, request or commitment;
- its evidence, qualification, exception and attribution;
- its reader function, when that is substantive: reassurance, caution, contrast, navigation, accountability or emphasis.

Group genuinely repeated propositions. A single clear expression can cover several occurrences, but two superficially similar sentences can do different jobs. A limitation and a request to communicate that limitation are separate. Keep both, possibly in one sentence. An enthusiastic opinion is content even without quantitative support. Do not delete it as filler or upgrade it to evidence.

**A preserved function does not require a preserved sentence.** Decide whether each piece of framing needs (a) its own sentence, (b) integration into an actual claim or action, or (c) no additional wording because the finished passage already performs that same function. A memo that directly reviews the supplied feedback usually need not also say `This memo reviews the feedback`. Preserve a special purpose, audience, status, attribution or disclaimer carried by that sentence in the relevant passage. Do not use this rule to drop an actual request, opinion, rationale or promise.

Consolidate repeated instructions by comparing **actor / action / object / occasion / force**. If two instructions differ only by occasion, one statement can explicitly cover both occasions. If they differ by action or force, retain both verbs and their original modality while sharing the object. A repeated rationale can be expressed once with the instruction it supports. This is semantic consolidation, not permission to summarize away distinct points.

For example, `The review must distinguish the temporary arrangement from permanent policy. The notice must also explain that distinction.` can become `The review must distinguish the temporary arrangement from permanent policy, a distinction the notice must explain.` The review's distinction and the notice's explanation remain separate obligations. Merely saying `The arrangement is temporary` loses both instructions.

For a short passage this map can be mental. For a dense, high-stakes or audited document record a source-to-candidate coverage table. Do not burden the normal output with it.

## 2. Bundle dependencies before choosing a route

Make relationship units before moving paragraphs. Bind a claim to the qualifier that governs its interpretation: a median to the missing distribution, an observation to the absence of causal evidence, a proposal to its approval status, and a deadline to its exception. These units can span more than one sentence. Their members need to stay close enough that the reader does not accept an unqualified claim and encounter its restriction much later.

Distinguish a local restriction from a document-wide restriction. Keep a result-specific limitation beside that result even if a later paragraph discusses general study limitations. A separate deployment-status paragraph must not pull the missing-distribution warning away from a reported median. Do not duplicate every qualification to solve a poor outline; reorganize its dependency unit first.

Then handle reader actions. Map `communicate`, `review`, `verify`, and `approve` separately from the facts they act on. Several actions may share one explicit object while preserving their different actors, timing and force. This avoids a procession of impersonal instructions without erasing any instruction. Do not merge distinct recipients or responsibilities into an invented collective `we`.

Synthetic example:

> The desk limit is 8. The desk limit is unchanged by longer opening hours. Reviewers should consider the limit when describing the trial. Notices should state the opening hours and desk limit separately.

Possible recomposition:

> Longer opening hours leave the desk limit at 8. Reviewers should account for that limit when describing the trial, and notices should state the hours and desk limit separately.

The factual relationship is stated once; the review and notice obligations remain distinct. A different actor, a weaker `may`, or a deadline in the source must survive too.

한국어에서도 사실과 독자 행위를 분리해 묶는다. `정원은 8명이다. 연장 운영 중에도 정원은 같다. 검토자는 운영안을 설명할 때 정원을 고려해야 한다. 안내문에는 시간과 정원을 각각 써야 한다.`는 `운영 시간을 늘려도 정원은 8명이다. 검토자는 이 한도를 고려해 운영안을 설명해야 하며, 안내문에는 운영 시간과 정원을 각각 밝혀야 한다.`처럼 쓸 수 있다. 주체와 의무가 다르면 무작정 하나의 지시로 합치지 않는다.

## 3. Choose the reader's route

Infer what the reader needs to understand or do from the source and request. Arrange information around that need instead of inheriting each source sentence's position.

| Actual document purpose | Useful route when the source supports it |
| --- | --- |
| Decision memo | Proposal or decision → material constraints → evidence and its limits → remaining decision |
| Technical result | Measured result and trade-off → test conditions → exclusions → deployment status or action |
| Explanation | The thing being explained → how its parts relate → worked example already supplied → boundary case |
| Request or update | Requested action or changed status → reason → timing, dependencies and courtesy |
| Customer support | Acknowledgment → immediate action when supported → findings and uncertainty → conditional remedy → clearly located next contact |
| Personal or creative text | The source's experience or argument in its intended order; retain purposeful suspense, digression and voice |

These are options, not templates to stamp on every document. Preserve a required abstract/section layout, chronology, argument order or quotation order. Do not move a conclusion forward when its scope becomes misleading without the preceding conditions.

Use paragraph boundaries to separate different reader tasks. For example, a survey's representativeness limit and an unresolved staffing condition deserve distinguishable treatment; a customer's immediate action should be easy to find amid diagnostic detail. Headings are useful only when they help navigation. Adding a heading to every fact group or turning prose into fragments is not inherently more natural.

If a long draft is structurally stuck, consider two brief outlines and choose the one with clearer dependencies. Draft only the useful candidate; there is no fixed candidate count. Let paragraphs differ in length because their jobs differ.

## 4. Draft with the chosen route

For recomposition, write the complete paragraph around its claims and relationships, then check the original. Do not walk down a formulaic passage replacing one phrase at a time. Preserve every substantive point without preserving every staging sentence. For selective restructuring, keep effective wording and connections while repairing the identified problem; local editing is appropriate when the weakness is local.

For a heavily formulaic draft, make the working notes independent of the original paragraph order: facts with dependencies, genuine opinions, reader actions, and protected wording. Compose a new passage from those notes and the editorial brief rather than keeping the old draft open as a sentence-by-sentence template. Reopen or consult the source for fidelity afterward. This is a drafting discipline, not a claim that a model can erase its context. Do not hide the source from the reviewer.

Make an actual compositional choice when warranted: change the entry point, regroup an argument, share the object of several instructions, or put an established viewpoint next to the evidence it evaluates. Moving intact sentences into a different order is not enough when their old setup and recap sentences remain. Do not change an effective structure merely to demonstrate activity.

Distinguish three levels of change:

1. **Lexical:** choose the precise ordinary word without varying a defined term.
2. **Syntactic:** change subjects, clause boundaries and verb structure to express the relationship directly.
3. **Discourse:** combine dispersed evidence and limits, remove duplicate framing, move the operative point, and give each paragraph a distinct job.

When several sentences share an empty frame, lexical edits alone are usually insufficient. `This highlights X` → `This underscores X` leaves the same frame. `검토할 필요가 있다` → `살펴볼 필요가 있다` leaves the same frame. Express X directly while retaining a real evaluation or recommendation and its original force.

Use source-supported specificity: name the already-stated action, object, condition or contrast. If no concrete detail exists, use a plain general sentence. Never fill the gap with an invented anecdote, motive, mechanism, sensory detail, author identity or fake uncertainty.

## 5. Give sentences different jobs

The reader should move from something established to something new. Replace vague chains of `this`, `these findings`, `이러한 점`, or `이는` with the actual referent when the link is unclear. Do not repeat an explicit noun when the subject is already obvious.

Let the key action carry the main clause. Put conditions close to what they limit. Use a longer sentence when evidence and qualification belong together; give an actual finding or action its own short sentence when it merits one. Do not alternate short/long sentences mechanically.

Inspect recurring paragraph tails. A conclusion that only names the preceding paragraph's lesson may be redundant. A new decision, request, inference, consequence, warning or stance is not redundant. Move a real instruction next to its object instead of deleting it.

Avoid replacing one generic style with another: a clipped punchline after every paragraph, a rhetorical question before each answer, forced conversational openings, or a stream of `not X but Y` contrasts remains formulaic.

## 6. Realize the structure in each language

### English

Start from the actor or object the reader is tracking. Use a direct predicate for the actual claim. Recover verbs from nominalizations without inventing an actor: `the team conducted a review of the logs` → `the team reviewed the logs`; actor-unknown `the logs were reviewed` stays passive if appropriate.

Keep register-specific rhythm. Formal prose can have contractions only when suitable; it does not require them. A straightforward sentence can be human without slang. Do not replace domain language with colorful synonyms.

Recover a concrete predicate rather than preserving an abstract commentary verb. `The estimate warrants consideration in the review` may become `Reviewers should consider the estimate` only if the recommendation and actor are supported; `the estimate is reliable` would invent a conclusion. Instead of adding a general transition about "limitations", connect the next specific limit to the subject already in focus. Keep the reader's attention on the topic, not the document's responsible handling of the topic.

Repeated frames such as `The purpose of this memo is to`, `These figures provide a starting point`, or `This distinction should remain visible` need a function check. A short memo rarely needs repeated descriptions of itself. When the sentence carries an actual instruction, attach that instruction to the relevant claim rather than erasing it.

### 한국어

영문 구문의 문장 경계를 유지한 채 어미만 바꾸지 않는다. 문단에서 독자가 따라갈 대상과 새로 알려 줄 내용을 정하고, 그 관계를 한국어 조사와 서술어로 다시 표현한다. 같은 주체는 분명할 때 생략하고, 주체가 바뀌거나 책임이 중요하면 명시한다.

`이는 …임을 보여준다`, `…라는 점을 고려해야 한다`, `…에 관한 논의가 필요하다`가 연이어 나오면, 정보 자체와 실제 검토·안내 요청을 구분한다. 정보는 직접 서술하고 요청은 대상 옆에 배치한다. `필요하다`를 지울 때 권고가 단정이나 명령으로 강해지지 않았는지 확인한다.

예: `공간의 수용 인원은 8명이다. 운영 시간을 늘려도 정원은 달라지지 않는다. 안내에서는 시간과 정원을 구분해 설명해야 한다.`는 `운영 시간을 늘리더라도 정원은 8명으로 같다. 안내에서는 운영 시간과 이 정원을 구분해 설명해야 한다.`처럼 쓸 수 있다. 조건부 연장을 이미 확정된 운영 변경으로 바꾸지 않는다. 정원·시간의 관계와 안내 의무가 모두 남는지 확인한다. 반면 `8명만 예약을 받는다`는 원문에 없는 예약 정책이므로 실패다.

상황에 맞는 문어체를 유지한다. 자연스럽게 보이려고 `-죠`, `-거든요`, 반말, 감탄을 임의로 섞지 않는다. 어미 빈도보다 문장 안에서 누가 무엇을 말하는지와 문장 사이의 논리를 먼저 고친다.

추상 명사만 줄이고 서술어를 남기는 수정에서 멈추지 않는다. `이 제약을 유지하며 판단할 수 있다`가 실제로 제약을 염두에 두라는 뜻이면 `이 제약을 염두에 두고 판단할 수 있다`로 풀어 쓴다. `별개의 판단으로 다루는 것이 적절하다`는 `따로 판단하는 것이 적절하다`처럼 쓸 수 있다. `적절하다`를 `해야 한다`로 올리거나 `할 수 있다`를 확정 사실로 바꾸지 않는다. 명확한 원문에 없던 `유의할 필요`, `고려할 필요`를 완충어로 덧붙이지 않는다.

### Mixed-language documents

Recompose each prose span in its own language, keeping shared entities and technical identifiers stable. Korean prose surrounding English terms still follows Korean syntax. Do not force both languages into matching sentence counts or identical paragraph boundaries unless alignment is part of the requested format.

## 7. Reject cosmetic success

Before accepting, answer these with concrete spans rather than a score:

- Did the chosen changes improve a concrete reader task while preserving the source features that already worked?
- If a formulaic structure needed rebuilding, did it actually change, or did most source sentences merely acquire synonyms?
- Does each paragraph add a distinct fact, relationship, stance or useful reader function?
- Has a necessary caution been stated once clearly rather than scattered or deleted?
- Can every distinct source point still be located in the candidate, including instructions about communicating a fact?
- Did a structural move separate a measure from its direct limitation or make the reader repeatedly reconstruct the same object across instructions?
- Did the rewrite invent a new meta-introduction (`There are also limits to what these measurements say`, `다음으로 검토할 점은`) where the next actual claim would connect more clearly on its own?
- Is the final voice appropriate to this document, without invented personality or excessive compression?
- Compared with the source, did editing introduce a new recurring frame, generic conclusion, punctuation habit or register drift? Keep a new construction when it improves the actual relationship; revise it when it merely replaces one formula with another. Make this comparison in both languages, without treating a pattern count as an authorship score.

No required change ratio, target length, sentence distribution or detector percentage applies. A good source may need little change. Detector disagreement does not license degrading a faithful sentence.

## Short recomposition example

### Shared purpose, different actions

Synthetic source:

> The repair report should include the inspection date. Including the inspection date helps the owner distinguish the current findings from earlier findings. The inspector should compare the current findings with the earlier findings when recommending repairs. This comparison is a useful basis for the recommendation.

Possible rewrite:

> The repair report should give the inspection date so the owner can tell the current findings from earlier ones. The inspector should use a comparison of those findings as the basis for recommending repairs.

Both actions, both actors, the recommendation's basis and the date's purpose remain. Their functions no longer need four separate explanatory sentences. This does not say that a comparison has already happened or that the repairs are approved.

한국어 예시:

> 수선 기록에는 접수일을 적을 필요가 있다. 접수일은 이번 요청과 이전 요청을 구분하는 데 도움이 된다. 담당자는 수선 범위를 제안할 때 두 요청을 비교하는 것이 좋다. 이 비교는 제안의 출발점으로 유용하다.

가능한 재작성:

> 이번 요청과 이전 요청을 구분할 수 있도록 수선 기록에 접수일을 적을 필요가 있다. 담당자는 두 요청을 비교한 내용을 바탕으로 수선 범위를 제안하는 것이 좋다.

권고의 강도와 두 기능을 남겼다. `접수일을 적어야 한다`, `비교를 마쳤다`, `수선 범위를 확정했다`로 바꾸면 실패다. 예시의 문체나 두 문장 구조를 다른 장르에 강제하지 않는다.

### Remove only redundant staging

Synthetic source:

> This note sets out the proposed studio trial. The trial would run for 4 weeks. Evening access would end at 21:00 on Wednesdays. It is necessary to consider the fact that a supervisor has not yet been assigned. This point needs to be resolved before the trial can start. The trial is temporary. Communications should make clear that it does not change the permanent schedule.

Candidate:

> The proposed 4-week studio trial would allow access until 21:00 on Wednesdays, but a supervisor still needs to be assigned before it can start. Notices should explain that the trial is temporary and leaves the permanent schedule unchanged.

Coverage: proposal + duration + day/time; unresolved supervisor + pre-start dependency; temporary scope + unchanged permanent schedule + notice instruction. The introductory self-description adds no distinct reader function here. The candidate is shorter because duplicated framing was removed, not because shortening was the objective.

If the source also says `I am excited about the trial`, that stance must remain. If it says `we should consider assigning a supervisor`, do not turn that recommendation into a mandatory pre-start dependency.
