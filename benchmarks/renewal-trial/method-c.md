# Edit the actual document

Rewrite Korean and English prose so its intended reader can use it without stumbling over generic phrasing. Structural revision is allowed. Preserve the language, speaker, register, substantive meaning, all facts and numbers, uncertainty, requirements and distinctions. Do not invent experiences, evidence, reasons or details. Do not optimize word counts, sentence statistics or detector scores.

Begin by reading the source as the recipient. Identify what this text is doing and where the reader actually has work to do. Decide which existing passages already do their job. Retain effective wording, local voice, transitions and perspective; a rewrite does not have to replace every sentence. Do not rebuild a functioning narrative into an abstract factual outline.

Let the document's job govern its form. A decision memo can lead with its proposal and organize unresolved responsibilities, supporting evidence and next actions so the recipient can find them. Use short headings or lists only if they genuinely clarify separate decisions; do not turn an essay or an email into a checklist. Preserve an explicit review remit and its decision stage in a natural location. Source qualifications must remain attached to the claims they limit, not become a detached stack of disclaimers.

Edit whole relationships between clauses. Find the person or object already being discussed; let the next sentence continue from that point. Replace abstract nominal phrasing with the concrete action the source actually states. Consolidate genuinely redundant explanation while retaining distinct functions, conditions, obligations and reasons. A paragraph should make progress, without having to announce its point and summarize it again.

For Korean, preserve the voice of a Korean speaker in the source's relationship and genre. Use natural predicates and topic continuity, without replacing every sentence ending or manufacturing fragments. For English, preserve the source's dialect and distance from the reader, and use ordinary idiomatic collocations. Neither language needs forced slang, punctuation bans, typos or invented anecdotes.

Finish the complete text. In a separate pass, compare every source assertion, qualification, instruction and reader function with the rewrite. Restore anything lost or altered; correct references affected by rearrangement. Then read for continuity and voice. Revise only a concrete defect. Keep the most faithful, useful version, even when retaining a good source sentence is the best edit.
