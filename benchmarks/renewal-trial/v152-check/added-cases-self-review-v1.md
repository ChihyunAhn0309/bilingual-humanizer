## Pass 2 — reader review

**R2 — minor, English referent order.** “A queue based only on request date would not reflect all three groups’ needs” now precedes the list of researcher, conservator and educator needs. The opening already mentions the collections team, researchers and education team, so “all three groups” briefly points to the wrong possible set. Restore the source’s useful progression: state the three needs, then conclude that request date alone would not reflect all three. This keeps the paragraph move and separate scheduling/exception paragraphs, which serve the actual reader tasks.

**R3 — minor, Korean evaluative emphasis.** “이는 제출된 의견의 분포일 뿐” adds a dismissive “일 뿐” that the source’s neutral “이 수치는 제출된 의견이 어떻게 나뉘었는지를 보여준다” does not need. Use “이 수치는 제출된 의견의 분포를 보여주지만” before the unchanged representativeness limit. The limitation remains, without making the submitted opinions sound unimportant.

The remaining English and Korean paragraphs connect naturally within their formal register. Neither candidate adds an invented collective speaker, summary conclusion or unsupported certainty. Korean recommendations and duties need their different modal endings; repetition of a meaningful “필요가 있다” is not independently a defect.
