Digitization queue for the next quarter

The collections team of Alderwick Museum, is suggesting that 60 percent of the next quarter's digitization facility would be set aside for objects which have already been requested by researchers. The remainder would be for fragile objects awaiting review for condition and the small number for education. This would be applied for one quarter, and then the possibility of it being reviewed again.

The queue at the museum has 184 objects, but these do not always indicate if there are still pending or otherwise requests have been answered through other means. Last week's sample of 25 objects reviewed consisted of 7 objects which seemed to require clarification. This sample was drawn from the oldest records so, in effect, may not be accurate for all records in the queue. We should confirm this before assigning dates for photography.

There are times when researchers need reliable information about the time of arrival, and conservators require enough time to handle objects which cannot safely be moved without preparation. Educators have requested images of ordinary household objects and these are answered less often, but support some planned curriculum teaching. A queue based only on request date would not address all of our needs.

I suggest that the registrar prepare a provisional schedule by 18 November and use the hours available with existing staff. Photography should start with those which have been confirmed and have no outstanding handling issues. Until then we require agreement on who will be able to apply for exceptions when a deadline, such as an exhibition, conflicts with the needs of the conservator. Until then, we require objects to remain on the provisional schedule with no promised completion date.
