# Additional development trial: detached composition

After the first four Sapling v1.5 observations, the two old English memos improve by 1.6 and 4.8 percentage points against v1.4, but a fresh English text worsens by 0.6. Blind editorial review slightly prefers v1.4 on all four old memos. These are insufficient grounds for a performance-improvement claim.

Test a distinct drafting architecture on the already exposed English cache and Korean library development cases: a source reader builds a complete semantic brief, then a fresh writer receives only that brief and source-backed voice information, not the source's full wording, old candidates or scores. A separate fidelity review retains access to the immutable original. This tests reduced sentence anchoring rather than more rewrite turns. It is adaptive development, not a heldout result.

No invented persona, experience, facts, slang, typo or hidden-character substitution is permitted. No meaning deletion for a score. Publish all attempted outcomes and no promise of detector acceptance. The v1.5 prospective comparison remains a separately labeled experiment; do not recast these further drafts as its frozen outputs.
