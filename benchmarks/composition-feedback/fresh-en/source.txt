I meant to make a short walking guide for a friend who was visiting on Sunday. On Wednesday I walked the route myself, starting at the station and ending at the old footbridge. I had estimated twenty minutes. It took thirty-five, partly because I stopped to check two street names. I did not time a second walk, so thirty-five minutes is a record of that trip, not a reliable estimate for everyone.

The map was useful until I reached the market. It showed a path behind the stalls, but a locked gate blocked the entrance when I got there. I cannot tell whether that gate is normally locked or whether I happened to arrive at the wrong time. In the guide, I should describe what I found and leave the usual access hours unconfirmed.

I liked the quieter road by the river, although its uneven pavement would make me hesitate to recommend it to someone using a wheelchair. I have not checked whether there is an accessible alternative. Before sending the guide, I need to check that point rather than present my preferred route as suitable for everyone.

I still want to send something useful by Sunday. A clear note about what I have and have not checked would help more than pretending that one walk settled every question.
