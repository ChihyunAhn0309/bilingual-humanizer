## Reader review after the fidelity pass

- The opening preserves the reflective genre and makes the still-required action easy to find. It does not become a set of walking directions or an invented message to the friend.
- "I have not checked that yet" has an immediate referent: whether an accessible alternative exists. This short status sentence is retained because the unfinished status and the obligation are distinct information.
- Moving the river-road material before the Wednesday account puts the preparation requirement first. The explicit "On Wednesday" introduces the retrospective evidence without inventing chronology between the river and market observations.
- The timing sentence is longer because date, endpoints, estimate, observation and partial explanation belong together. The following two sentences keep the evidence restriction readable. No target sentence-length distribution was used.
- The final sentence joins the market reporting duty and the supplied concluding comparison with a semicolon. The second clause names the checked/unchecked distinction across the guide rather than falsely claiming the market question is the only unresolved item.
- First-person register remains plain and restrained. No slang, fabricated confession, new feeling, intentional error or hidden character was added. The text still uses substantive contrasts because the source's evidence limits and closing evaluation depend on them.

No concrete reader-quality defect remained that justified changing the frozen draft.
