## Dependencies kept together

- K03–K05: schedule is conditional trial scope, with no implication of permanent approval.
- K17–K19: initial and later volunteer checks remain explicit; acknowledging interest does not satisfy availability.
- K20–K22: the capacity number remains bound to simultaneous quiet-room access, not daily library use.
- K06–K13: all categories, interest interpretation, representative scope, minority consideration, and response reasons stay attached to the counts.
- K14–K16: the preference/visit and causal limits remain distinct but adjacent.
- K23–K25: explaining facts publicly and checking how the proposal reads are separate actions. The document-review recommendation is not silently strengthened into an obligation.
