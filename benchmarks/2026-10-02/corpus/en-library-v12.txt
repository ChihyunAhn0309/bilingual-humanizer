The proposed community library pilot would extend opening hours until 20:00 on Tuesday and Thursday evenings for 6 weeks. It should be clearly identified as temporary: it does not establish a permanent policy, and discussions of future hours should keep that distinction clear.

Volunteer availability remains unresolved and needs to be addressed before the proposed evening schedule can become a workable plan, rather than left until the pilot has begun. The quiet room would still have a capacity of 12. That limit should inform descriptions of what the pilot could offer, and communications should distinguish the extra time the building would be open from the space available for quiet use.

Of the 42 feedback forms received, 28 support later hours, 9 prefer the current schedule, and 5 express no preference. The distribution of submitted opinions gives the review a useful starting point, but it does not capture every library user's needs. The review should consider the preference for later opening alongside the reasons some respondents prefer the current schedule, without treating either group as representative of everyone.

Support on a form expresses a preference; it is not evidence of a resulting visit. The available information does not establish that longer hours cause attendance to rise, and the review should retain that limit when weighing the proposal. A decision can acknowledge the interest in later hours while requiring clarity about volunteer coverage. Any permanent change should remain a separate discussion.
