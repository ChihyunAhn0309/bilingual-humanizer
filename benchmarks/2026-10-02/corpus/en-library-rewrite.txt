The proposed pilot would keep the community library open until 20:00 on Tuesday and Thursday evenings for 6 weeks. This memo considers the feedback received and the questions that need answers before the proposal becomes a workable plan. The pilot would be temporary; it would not establish permanent opening hours. That distinction should guide any discussion of the library's future schedule.

Of the 42 feedback forms received, 28 support later hours, 9 prefer the current schedule, and 5 express no preference. The figures show the balance of submitted opinions and offer a starting point for the review. They do not give a complete account of every library user's needs. Interest in later hours deserves consideration, as do the reasons some respondents prefer the existing schedule. Neither group should be taken to speak for all users.

There is also a limit to what this interest tells us about attendance. A preference expressed on a form does not show that the respondent would visit during the extra hours, and the available information does not establish that longer hours cause attendance to rise. This limitation should remain clear as the proposal is assessed.

Volunteer availability is still unresolved and needs attention before the pilot starts. Evening coverage is a practical requirement for running the proposed schedule, not a detail to leave until it is under way. The quiet room's capacity of 12 also limits what the pilot could offer: staying open later would not create more space. Any communications should distinguish the hours of access to the building from the space available for quiet use.

A decision can recognize the interest in later hours while requiring clarity on volunteer coverage. It should also keep any permanent change to opening hours as a separate question.
