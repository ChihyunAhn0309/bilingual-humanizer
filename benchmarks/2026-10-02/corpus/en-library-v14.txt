The proposed community library pilot would extend opening hours until 20:00 on Tuesday and Thursday evenings for 6 weeks. This memo reviews the feedback and the questions still to be resolved before the proposal can become a workable plan. The pilot should be clearly defined as temporary: it would not establish a permanent policy. Discussions of future hours should preserve that distinction and treat any permanent change separately.

Volunteer availability remains unresolved and cannot simply be left until after the pilot begins. A decision on the pilot would still require clarity about coverage. The quiet room's capacity would remain at 12 despite the longer hours. The review should take that limit into account when describing what the pilot could offer, and communications should distinguish the time the building would remain open from the space available for quiet use.

The library received 42 feedback forms: 28 support later hours, 9 prefer the current schedule, and 5 express no preference. This distribution gives the review a useful starting point, but does not capture every library user's needs. The preference for later hours deserves consideration alongside respondents' reasons for retaining the current schedule, without treating either group as speaking for everyone.

A decision can acknowledge the interest in later hours, but the review should make clear that support on a feedback form is an expression of preference, not evidence of a resulting visit. The available information does not establish that longer opening hours cause attendance to rise.
