# Holdout blind review

## Scope and verdict

This review uses only the six supplied files in `holdout-review/en/` and `holdout-review/ko/`: each language's source, A, and B. The texts are treated as synthetic; this review makes no authorship claims. Preferences below concern the supplied texts, with no knowledge of their provenance.

**English: A, slight preference. Korean: A, slight preference.** Both versions in both languages preserve the substantive content and are usable. Neither language has a large quality gap. No material factual, obligation, or release-condition defect was found.

Here, *slight* means a discernible editorial preference among acceptable versions, not a failure of the other version. A material defect would change a decision, obligation, factual claim, eligibility rule, or prerequisite.

## English

### Fidelity and decision content

Both versions retain the museum and proposing team; the proposed 60 percent allocation; the remaining capacity for fragile objects and education; and the one-quarter duration followed by reconsideration. Neither turns the proposal into an approved allocation.

Both retain the queue of 184 objects, the review of 25 entries last week, the 7 entries that *appeared* to need clarification, and the oldest-record sampling caveat. Neither generalizes the sample rate to the whole queue or establishes that those 7 entries are definitively faulty. B's instruction to confirm “requests whose status is unclear” is a reasonable explicit rendering of the source's “those requests,” not a new audit requirement.

Both preserve the three stakeholder needs, the registrar's recommended provisional schedule by 18 November, use of existing staff hours, the handling restriction on the first photography work, and the unresolved authority over exhibition/conservation conflicts. The instruction that disputed objects remain scheduled without a promised completion date survives intact.

### Material defects and fixes

**Material defects: none in A or B. No mandatory factual repair is needed.**

If exact semantic restraint is the priority, B would benefit from restoring the source's conditional force in the request-date judgment and the adequacy qualifier on conservation time. Naming the three groups before referring to “all three” would improve local cohesion. These are minor fidelity or readability refinements, not reasons to reject B. No necessary correction was identified for A.

## Korean

### Fidelity and decision content

Both versions preserve 누리결연구소 운영지원팀 as proposer, the proposed start next month and six-week duration, and the purpose of giving teams access when needed and allowing others to use newly available time. Neither reframes full booking utilization as the objective.

Both retain 8 registered devices, 17 same-day cancellations last month, the limits of those records, the teams' maintenance explanation, and incomplete supporting maintenance records. Both retain the judgment against restricting a specific team's access on those figures alone. Neither treats the teams' explanation as independently verified or interprets missing attachments as proof of misuse.

Both preserve the proposed four-hour continuous booking limit for a team using the same device; reporting estimated duration and interruptibility at the application stage for longer measurements; the distinct development/analysis team needs; the proposed cancellation deadline of two hours before use; separate recording of unforeseeable reasons; and the commitment not to impose cancellation-count penalties during the trial. Weekly monitoring and collection of inconvenience reports are unchanged.

Both preserve the unresolved decision-maker and criteria for prioritizing urgent requests, their status as prerequisites to starting the trial, and each team's request to submit opinions by this Friday. Neither treats the anticipated start next month as overriding those prerequisites.

### Material defects and fixes

**Material defects: none in A or B. No mandatory factual repair is needed.**

B could restore “다만” or an equally clear exception cue before the unforeseeable-cancellation reasons, particularly if readers will use the memo to implement the rule. Its final prerequisite/status sentences could be integrated to avoid returning to their unresolved status after stating the start condition. The “짧은 빈 시간” phrase can also be smoothed without changing content. These are minor improvements; the four-hour rule, cancellation treatment, start prerequisites, and Friday submission request already remain intact. No necessary correction was identified for A.
